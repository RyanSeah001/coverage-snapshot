How to get the data from ePartner
  1. Open Customer Service → Policy Enquiry and search for the client.
  2. Click a policy number to open its Policy Detail Information page.
  3. Either press Ctrl+S and save as "Webpage, HTML only", or press Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C and paste the text above.
  4. Repeat for each in-force policy. Optionally add the Policy Listing page too, so surrendered or withdrawn policies are listed.
